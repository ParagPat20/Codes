1. MODULE COMPARISON SUMMARY (at 100 Hz Transmission Speed)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Module Model                 | Signal RSSI      | Packet Loss % | Link Quality
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C6   | -60 to -70 dBm   | 0.05 %        | EXCELLENT
Standard ESP32 DevKit v1     | -70 to -80 dBm   | 1.70 %        | GOOD / FAIR
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. FREQUENCY BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS (Seeed XIAO ESP32-C6)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Packet Transmission Speed    | Observed Packet Loss % | Cause / Status
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
100 Hz (10.0 ms interval)    | 0.05 %                 | Optimal Airtime Window
500 Hz (2.0 ms interval)     | ~ 50 %                 | Airtime & Buffer Saturation
1 kHz  (1.0 ms interval)     | ~ 70 %                 | RF Frame Overlap (>1.2ms)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. KEY FINDINGS & ENGINEERING INSIGHTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C6 demonstrated ~34x lower packet loss (0.05% vs 1.7%)
  and ~10 dBm stronger signal reception compared to standard ESP32 DevKit boards.
- 10 dBm higher RSSI represents approximately 10x greater RF signal power.
- High packet loss at 500Hz and 1kHz is physically caused by 237-byte frame airtime
  duration (~1.2ms per packet) exceeding the transmission interval timing.
